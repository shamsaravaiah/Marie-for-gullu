Drop your pet pictures in THIS folder.

Easiest: just drop your raw images here (any name, any format — PNG/JPG/etc.)
and tell Claude. Claude will cut out the backgrounds and rename them to the
final files the app needs:

    images/cat.png      (black & white / tuxedo cat)
    images/shiba.png    (Shiba)
    images/husky.png    (Husky)

These should be transparent PNGs (background removed). If you'd rather prepare
them yourself, save them with those exact names.

Best results: a SIDE-VIEW, FULL-BODY pet on a plain/white background.
The app sizes them to ~220 x 200 and rests them on the ground.

If a file is missing, the app falls back to the built-in drawing for that pet.
